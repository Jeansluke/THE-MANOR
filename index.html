Martellos Manor

“The Horrors from 19th of june 21:47 2025 will never be forgotten.”

⚠ Work in progressThis article is being updated as we speak. A lot of the information here is true.
⚠ Content warningContains descriptions of disturbing events and violent content related to the incidents at Martellos Manor on June 19, 2025.

Martellos Manor is a private residence owned by an individual known only as “Martellos.” It gained notoriety after the events of June 19, 2025, at 21:47 (9:47 PM), and has since become the subject of intense speculation and investigation in online communities.

The property appears to be a secluded estate, though its exact location is unknown to the public. What is known comes primarily from chat logs and testimonies of people who claim to have been present.

The June 19th Incident

On June 19, 2025, at approximately 21:47, a series of disturbing events occurred at Martellos Manor, later referred to as “The Horrors.” The incident involved multiple individuals, including someone called “Fipters” and “the bald man.”

The Horrors from 19th of june 21:47 2025 will never be forgotten.— Manor description

According to recovered chat logs, the situation escalated quickly, with references to weapons, threats, and a general atmosphere of terror. The exact nature of what happened remains unclear, but witness accounts suggest:

  • Multiple individuals in conflict
  • References to weapons and violence
  • A mysterious “bald man” who appeared to know the location
  • Attempts to fix some kind of equipment malfunction

Manor Layout & Architecture

Recent reconnaissance has revealed unsettling details about the construction and room arrangement. The building seems designed with specific purposes in mind, though the architect's identity is unknown.

The Roof Situation

Aerial observations confirm the roof contains no traditional rooms or living spaces, raising questions about the building's intended use and structural integrity.

The Secret Chair Room

Most disturbing is the “goon room”: a secret chamber containing nothing but a single chair at its center, reached through hidden passages and serving an unknown purpose.

⚠ Investigation alertThe purpose of the chair room remains unclear. Witnesses report an unsettling atmosphere and nicknamed it the “goon corner.” Its sparse furnishing and hidden location suggest a specific, undisclosed function.

Chat logs reference the space with phrases like “goon corner” and “IT IS THAT ROOM MAM,” suggesting both familiarity with and unease about it.

Current Status

The manor remains active per recent reports, though its location is still unknown. The events of June 19th have entered internet folklore, and the phrase “The Horrors from 19th of june 21:47 2025 will never be forgotten” is now tied to the incident.

Martellos continues to invite visitors and discuss architectural modifications, and the chair room adds a new layer of mystery. No official investigation has been confirmed, and what truly happened remains a mystery.
